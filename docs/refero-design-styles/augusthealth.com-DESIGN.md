# August Health — circles of human optimism

## Overview

A welcoming, editorial technology style that puts people around the product rather than behind it. Fine, regular-weight serif headlines sit beside a direct contemporary sans, with white and warm cream interrupted by violet, leaf green, orange and pink. Circular portraits, gently staged interface imagery and bright proof cards give serious operational software a buoyant, humane character. The overall density is airy, with compact information inside rounded panels.

### Essentials

- Editorial confidence without typographic force: regular Reckless Neue headlines at 64px/1 and 48px/1.1, with normal tracking, paired with Saans body text at 16px/1.6.
- A clear center surrounded by life: a 900px opening with a 560px text column, 468px circular portraits and interface fragments clipped by the perimeter; use original people photography or meaningful interface fragments rather than empty decoration.
- Floating, soft-edged controls: a 1160px translucent navigation capsule, 40px backdrop blur, and blue pill actions with 16px 40px padding and 24px/1.1 labels.
- Color arrives in chapters, not a uniform wash: white and cream carry the product catalog, pale gradients stay inside 24px-radius cards, and violet bands and saturated 376px proof cards provide deliberate interruptions.
- Software is made tactile and approachable: interface panels sit upright on pale tinted grounds, softly shadowed and sometimes faded into the card base; the product image occupies most of each 572px-wide tile.
- Circles recur at both scales: large perimeter discs return as thin colored rings on dark violet, while 48px arrow circles and dot-built pictograms carry the same round geometry into cards.
- End with a change of register: a spacious closing statement leads into an 829px two-green footer with a 530px emblem panel and a structured link directory. Use an original emblem or simple geometric symbol as the oversized anchor.

## Typography

- **Reckless Neue** — Headlines, card titles, oversized statistics and editorial quotes. Fine, high-contrast contemporary serif with open, personable shapes; always regular weight in the marketing hierarchy. If unavailable, use **DM Serif Display**. Use 400; check wrapping because the substitute has heavier fine strokes. Font stack: "Reckless Neue", "DM Serif Display", Georgia, serif.
- **Saans** — Paragraphs, navigation, action labels, testimonial text and attribution. Clean contemporary grotesk with a generous x-height and conversational texture. If unavailable, use **DM Sans**. Use 400–600 with normal tracking. Font stack: "Saans", "DM Sans", Arial, sans-serif.

**Notes:**

- Keep the serif at weight 400 even in the largest statement. Hierarchy comes from size, line wrapping and color reversal, not bold display text.
- Normal letter spacing is shared across the hierarchy; do not impose tight startup-style tracking or widely spaced uppercase labels.
- Main opening support is 24px/1.4; recurring section support is smaller at 20px/1.6. Testimonials use the larger sans recipe rather than the serif by default.

## Layout

**Density:** Airy at section scale, comfortable inside cards; substantial pauses separate dense product and proof groups.

### Spacing

**Spacing system:**

- **horizontal step** 8px, horizontal — Dominant horizontal spacing unit for gutters, padding and repeated groups; 12px and 26px are deliberate exceptions.

| Value | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 8px | Attribution and compact inline gaps. |
| 12px | Pictogram-to-heading spacing. |
| 16px | Grid gutters and heading-to-description separation. |
| 24px | Capability-row padding and medium separation. |
| 26px | Product-card heading block to arrow separation. |
| 32px | Default card padding, quote spacing and copy-to-action gap. |
| 40px | Global side padding. |
| 64px | Heading-to-grid separation and medium section pauses. |
| 80px | Logo-band vertical padding. |
| 96px | Footer top padding and selected grid introductions. |
| 128px | Major section top and bottom padding. |
| 160px | Large split-panel chapter padding. |

### Containers and grids

- **main container**: max width 1160px · side padding 40px. Centered desktop content; at 1440px this leaves 140px margins.
- **opening text**: max width 560px. Centered two-line landing headline and support.
- **section text**: max width 768px. Centered heading and explanation for proof and feature introductions.
- **product grid**: max width 1160px · 2 columns · gap 16px. Two equal 572px product cards; images dominate the lower portion.
- **proof grid**: max width 1160px · 3 columns · gap 16px. Three 376px proof or quote cards; customer libraries use masonry heights rather than equal-height rows.
- **capability frame**: max width 832px · 1 column · gap 32px · side padding 32px. Stack of colorful split-text capability rows inside a rounded line frame.
- **FAQ column** (only for product FAQs): max width 800px · 1 column · gap 20px. Single centered list of bordered questions.

## Imagery and graphics

### Circular people and product constellation

**Use:** Signature opening composition.

**Observed:** Large green, orange, blue and pink circles are partially clipped by all four viewport edges. Some contain smiling, naturally lit people isolated against saturated color; others contain enlarged translucent interface fragments. The central copy remains untouched by the discs.

**Transfer:** Use relevant people at work plus fragments of the new product. If portraits are unavailable, alternate real workflow details and original simple interface panels within the discs.

**Build:** Make an absolutely positioned overflow-hidden scene behind the text. Arrange approximately eight 468px circles with border-radius:50% around the perimeter, leaving the central 560px column clear. Fill with leaf green, orange, blue and pink; clip portrait cutouts and upright interface panels inside the discs. Place a broad shallow cream SVG curve over the lower edge.

### Outline circle field

**Use:** Signature dark closing composition and editorial counterpart to the colorful opening.

**Observed:** The same oversized perimeter circles become thin green, blue, orange and rose outlines on violet. The center is a plain stage for a white serif statement.

**Transfer:** Repeat the circular grammar without portraits, keeping the copy area open.

**Build:** Reuse the 468px perimeter circle positions as transparent SVG circles with approximately 1px strokes. Clip circles beyond the section bounds, use the accent colors, and place the text layer above them.

### Upright interface stills

**Use:** Product tiles and feature demonstrations.

**Observed:** White or cream interface panels stay front-facing, never tilted in perspective. Panels have soft warm shadows and rounded corners. A focused dialog or small table floats within the lower card area; some lower edges fade gently into the pale background.

**Transfer:** Show a believable, concise workflow with readable major labels. If full product media is unavailable, construct a single realistic task panel with restrained rows and controls instead of a blank rectangle.

### Dotted pictograms and halos

**Use:** Signature card headings, saturated proof cards and subtle proof-area backing.

**Observed:** Small symbols are formed from round dots in category colors. Behind proof groups, a faint regular dot field is strongest near the cards and fades toward the edges.

**Transfer:** Create simple recognizable geometric symbols, keeping the dot language consistent across the page.

**Build:** In SVG, place equal circular dots on a regular grid, masking them into a simple ring, arrow, heart or block shape; size the finished symbol to 32px or 48px. For the backing, use a repeated radial-gradient of tiny warm-gray dots at low opacity, then mask with a broad radial fade so no hard rectangular texture edge appears.

### Customer interview

**Use:** Large featured proof story.

**Observed:** A naturally lit, waist-up interview portrait in a real interior fills a 960px-wide video panel. A translucent round play control sits at center. Beneath it, a white panel holds a serif quotation and portrait attribution.

**Transfer:** Use an authentic speaker or a strong editorial portrait with a concise quote; maintain the warm, professional light rather than glossy stock-photo staging.

### Culture photography mosaic

**Only for:** recruiting pages

**Use:** People-focused opening strip and mid-page culture gallery.

**Observed:** Candid offsites, outdoor group activities, conversations and everyday team artifacts appear in rounded rectangular frames. The opening uses a horizontal strip; the gallery uses three smaller images above two wider images with 16px gutters.

**Transfer:** Use real collaboration and the texture of daily work, with natural colors and varied framing rather than uniformly posed headshots.

### Product dome and warm glow

**Only for:** product-detail openings

**Use:** Stage for a nearly full-width interface hero.

**Observed:** A peach circular dome rises behind a centered product headline and 1160px-wide interface. An orange glow supports the lower interface area.

**Transfer:** Use one clear overview of the new product, with the same warm arch and upright presentation.

**Build:** Center the Product dome behind the hero, starting 100px below the top. Place the interface above it. Add a 1090px by 681px orange backing layer under the image, blur it by 40px and translate it downward by 40%; contain the effect within the opening.

### Icons

**Observed:** Round-dot category pictograms are 32px or 48px. Navigation uses small down chevrons; card arrows are fine straight-line right arrows, often inside 48px outlined or white raised circles. Play controls use blue triangular fills.

**Transfer:** Use dotted geometry for category identity and thin conventional strokes for navigation; do not replace both with a single heavy filled icon library.

## Section recipes

How recurring sections are built.

### First screen: Circular stage opening

**Observed:** A white 900px first screen is framed by large perimeter discs, leaving a calm central 560px text column. Behind-to-front layers are white canvas, colorful circles with people and interface fragments, centered text and action, then fixed Floating navigation and a narrow news capsule beneath it. The 64px serif headline wraps into two lines around the middle of the viewport; 24px support follows after 16px, then Primary button after 32px. Headline, support, action and substantial portions of the surrounding circles all fit within the first 900px. A shallow cream curve enters at the bottom; the trust-logo band lies below the fold.

**Transfer:** Frame an original promise with relevant people and focused product details, maintaining an uncluttered center. Recruiting openings use outlined circles and a photo strip entering the lower screen; company narratives use white 48px serif text on violet rings; product-detail openings use Product dome and a large interface instead of perimeter portraits.

**Avoid:** Do not turn this into a conventional left-copy/right-dashboard hero, put a giant disc behind the headline, or let the perimeter media collide with the action.

**Text style:** `display` — Reckless Neue 400 · 64px/1

### Category and heading pair

**Observed:** Section label precedes a centered heading-lg by 32px. Support follows by 16px to 24px. The text block is usually 560px to 768px wide and the next content group begins 64px later.

**Transfer:** Use the label to identify the chapter, the serif heading for the outcome and the support for a concrete explanation.

**Text style:** `heading-lg` — Reckless Neue 400 · 48px/1.1

### Pastel product catalog

**Observed:** Product grid holds Product cards in two columns with 16px gutters. The title and description are compact at top while interface imagery takes most of the lower tile. A compact company variant uses three columns of text-led 384px by 207px tiles.

**Transfer:** Use a small set of distinct capabilities with consistent staging, reserving the compact three-column variant for summaries.

### Violet capability circuit

**Observed:** Violet chapter carries a centered white heading and support, followed by the 832px Capability frame with 32px inset and 48px corners. Capability rows form a stack with 32px gaps. Colored perimeter paths terminate in small round dots, then a shallow curved boundary returns the page to cream.

**Transfer:** Explain a few complementary advantages in a graphic framed group; keep the linework secondary to the split title and explanation.

### Featured story and proof rail

**Observed:** Cream proof band begins with a centered editorial lead and a Customer interview. Below it a horizontal rail mixes Proof cards with 32px gaps and visible clipped neighboring cards. Two 64px circular previous/next controls sit centered below; a faint dotted halo grounds the rail.

**Transfer:** Combine a human account with several distinct outcomes, using a rail when content warrants exploration rather than squeezing all cards into a narrow grid.

### Split statement and supporting panels

**Observed:** A 64px white serif statement fills a solid rounded panel beside a stack of Outlined panels. The recruiting version uses violet and five benefit rows; product capability chapters use green or blue and several explanatory cards.

**Transfer:** Use this asymmetric treatment for a strong thematic promise with concise supporting reasons, not for every feature group.

### Ring-framed closing call

**Observed:** A 900px Violet chapter repeats the Outline circle field. A centered white heading-lg within 688px makes a substantial statement; a Compact button sits 32px beneath. The Two-green footer follows immediately.

**Transfer:** Close with a memorable differentiator or invitation that deserves a generous stage. Shorter editorial pages instead use a cream call with 128px vertical padding; detailed product pages can use a blue reverse-text call with customer marks, then related-product cards before the shared footer.

**Text style:** `heading-lg` — Reckless Neue 400 · 48px/1.1

### Proof-library masonry

**Only for:** customer proof libraries

**Observed:** A cream opening uses a 48px centered serif title and 20px support above a three-column wall of Quote cards. Saturated statistic cards interrupt the translucent-white rhythm. At the end, a centered invitation to share leads across a shallow curved cream-to-white boundary into a separate conversion call and outlined topic capsules.

**Transfer:** Let quote length drive card heights and mix in verified outcomes; use an authentic referral invitation before the final product action.

### Recruiting action ending

**Only for:** recruiting pages

**Observed:** After the photo mosaic, a centered 48px serif heading introduces an embedded role directory with compact filters, grouped job links and short metadata. The page ends directly with the Two-green footer rather than adding a demo CTA.

**Transfer:** End a recruiting journey with the actual application destinations, allowing the functional directory to be plainer than the surrounding editorial sections.

### Editorial list and soft close

**Only for:** company narratives and news pages

**Observed:** The mission opening is followed by a bright blue text panel, a two-column origin story, a compact product summary and a single-column News row list. The list transitions through a shallow curve into a cream closing action with 48px serif heading and 20px support before the Two-green footer.

**Transfer:** Balance a focused company belief with concrete history and current activity; keep the closing call short.

## Writing

Warm, assured and unusually human for operational software. Strong claims are expressed in plain language, then made credible through a workflow, a person's voice or a measurable result.

**Do not copy:** Do not reuse August Health names, logos, founder affiliations, certification marks, customer identities, quoted endorsements or numerical claims. The examples illustrate voice only; new content needs its own truthful facts and authorized assets.

- **Addressing the reader:** Use you and your for the reader's work, we for shared beliefs and commitments, and the product name when explaining an actual capability.
- **Rhythm:** Short, outcome-led headlines alternate with one- or two-sentence explanations. Occasional longer serif statements carry beliefs; informal enthusiasm belongs mainly in authentic quotations.

| Role | Pattern | Typical length | Examples of the pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero headline | Name the product category and the human response or outcome. | 5–9 words | “The EHR platform caregivers love” |
| Supporting line | Explain what the platform enables and for whom, without a list of technical abstractions. | 12–24 words | “[Brand] is the AI-enabled platform for better care and operations in senior living communities.” |
| Section headline | Join a concrete design quality to the reader's working aspiration. | 5–12 words | “Designed for forward-thinking, care-obsessed operators” |
| Capability title | Use balanced phrasing or a practical verb phrase. | 2–7 words | “Accessible data, actionable insights”<br>“Stay audit-ready, 24/7” |
| Primary action | Direct verb plus a clear destination or interaction. | 2–4 words | “Request demo”<br>“View open roles”<br>“Book a demo” |
| Closing headline | Make a brief, human invitation rather than restating every benefit. | 4–12 words | “Let’s get to work”<br>“Ready for [Brand]? You are in great company.” |

**Words and phrasing to use:** purpose-built; easy-to-use; thoughtfully designed; empower; real-time; proactive; better; your team; workflows

**Avoid:** revolutionary disruption; synergy; 10x everything; dominate; frictionless magic; robotic jargon

**Rules:**

- Tie each capability to a real action or improved working day.
- Use paired contrasts sparingly: reactive versus proactive, paperwork versus clarity, simple enough versus powerful enough.
- Keep CTA language literal and consistent across the page.
- Let testimonials retain the speaker's personality rather than rewriting every voice into corporate prose.
- Use verified quantified proof beside human testimony, not instead of it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep headline serif weight at 400 and tracking normal across 64px, 48px and 32px roles.
- Protect the 560px opening text column from the perimeter discs.
- Use 16px product-grid gutters and 32px internal card padding.
- Let pastel tiles fade into the shared warm-white base by the midpoint.
- Give proof numbers the 64px serif display treatment rather than a bold sans counter.
- Repeat the circular motif in 48px arrows and thin ring fields as well as the opening artwork.
- Use 128px section padding to create pauses between dense capability and proof groups.
- Finish with a distinct two-green directory and an oversized original emblem.

### Don't

- Do not use bold grotesk display headlines in place of the fine regular serif.
- Do not tighten every headline with negative letter spacing; normal tracking is part of the open texture.
- Do not convert the pastel product tiles into saturated colored feature cards; reserve saturated fills for proof and statement chapters.
- Do not give every white panel a dark gray shadow; use warm low-alpha shadows only for genuinely raised elements.
- Do not use sharp-cornered rectangular CTAs; actions are fully rounded pills.
- Do not tilt interface images into dramatic 3D perspective; their front-facing clarity is characteristic.
- Do not make proof-library cards equal height; quote length determines the masonry rhythm.
- Do not replace the footer with a small monochrome link bar; its two-green split is a major closing gesture.

## Motion and responsive

### Motion

**Observed:**

- Primary and compact actions declare 0.3s transitions; the large action lifts 3px and the compact navigation action scales to 1.02 on hover.
- Case-study cards declare a 10px hover lift with a deeper warm shadow.
- Logo tickers declare linear infinite loops with 100s, 160s or 220s durations.
- The footer is sticky beneath a relatively positioned main layer, producing a layered reveal at the page ending.

**Transfer:**

- Keep interaction movement subtle and concentrated in pills, arrows and proof cards.
- If using a logo rail, preserve the slow linear character rather than a fast marquee.

**Unknown:**

- Exact entrance choreography, circle animation paths and timing relationships are not established.
- Focus-visible behavior is known for some navigation and carousel controls, not every component.

### Responsive

**Observed:**

- Styles include breakpoint families around 479px, 767px, 991px and 1240px.

**Transfer:**

- On narrow layouts, preserve the clear central promise by clipping or reducing perimeter circles rather than shrinking the entire desktop scene.
- Stack product and split-capability panels when their copy and media no longer fit comfortably; retain the distinction between large statement panels and supporting rows.
- Keep the oversized footer emblem as a distinct panel when the directory stacks.

**Unknown:**

- Exact mobile font sizes, navigation replacement, grid collapse points and footer arrangements are not established; these are adaptation recommendations.

## How to use this guide

1. Start from the Essentials and the first-screen recipe; where the guide offers variants, use the default named for a new marketing page.
2. Plan the page before building: map your content onto the section recipes and page flow, then check the plan against the Do's and Don'ts.
3. Build with the tokens and component specs; recreate signature graphics with their build recipes instead of generic decoration, and use the named font substitutes if a font is unavailable.
4. Render the page at desktop and mobile widths, compare it with the Essentials and the Don'ts, and fix the differences before you finish.
