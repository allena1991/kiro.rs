# Contractbook — big shapes, human scribbles

## Overview

A light, approachable software-marketing style that makes serious workflows feel manageable. Bold grotesk headings and orderly interface demonstrations sit inside soft, rounded panels, punctuated by electric blue, golden yellow, pale sky blue and lilac. Hand-drawn stickers, scribbles and real people temper the geometric structure; spacious storytelling gives way to a dense, dark directory footer.

### Essentials

- A confident promise sits inside a friendly white shell: 48px/1.25 bold grotesk, normal tracking, a 1156px-wide panel with 40px corners and 59.5px inner padding; a geometric blue-and-sky backdrop remains visible around it.
- Conversion is compact and rounded, not rectangular: yellow primary pills use 14px/1 bold text, 15.8px 28px padding and a companion 1px ink-outline trial pill separated by 14px.
- Color arrives in bold islands within a predominantly pale page: white and #f0f0ec panels carry most reading, while #1009f6 bands, #ffba09 proof tiles and #add3e5 media backings provide concentrated contrast.
- Product evidence is staged as a cheerful collage: rounded interface windows occupy roughly half a feature row, with colored geometric framing and irregular ink doodles. Include the framing and hand-drawn accents even when the substitute media is a simplified workflow diagram.
- Proof is a two-storey object: a 280px-high saturated quote tile over a 280px-high smoke-colored explanation and attribution tile, both with 24px corners and 48px padding; 28px/1.3 bold pull quotes lead.
- The closing invitation is personal rather than monumental: a white two-column panel pairs reassuring copy and a yellow edge scribble with a pale inset form, then hands off to a full-width #1a1a1a directory footer.

**Notes:**

- Normal letter spacing is consistent across the hierarchy; the character comes from broad bold forms, not compressed tracking.
- Default display is bold throughout. Regular setup plus bold italic payoff belongs to a product-opening variant, not every heading.

### Containers and grids

- **main-container**: max width 1200px · side padding 22px. Centered outer container. At desktop this yields a 1156px usable span with broad page margins.
- **hero-content**: max width 1037px. Opening copy, actions and media share an inset alignment within the white shell; headline measure is about 536px.
- **section-intro**: max width 560px. Centered chapter headline with compact paragraph below.
- **feature-pair**: 2 columns · gap 59.5px. Equal text and media columns inside the rounded supporting-video panel.
- **closing-pair**: 2 columns · gap 56px. Copy column and slightly wider form column, with a 1:1.25 relationship.
- **story-grid** (only for story indexes and pricing plans): 3 columns · gap 28px. Three equal 367px cards across the usable container.

## Imagery and graphics

### Geometric opening backdrop

**Use:** Signature framing behind the white opening shell.

**Observed:** Flat electric-blue and sky-blue polygons cross at steep diagonals. The artwork spans the viewport behind the inset shell, ending with rounded lower corners; a yellow squiggle peeks from an exposed edge.

**Transfer:** Retain the large-scale geometry even without product media; the white shell should hide most of the art.

**Build:** Place an absolute full-width layer about 768px tall behind the opening, clipped to 40px bottom corners. Use an SVG sky rectangle with oversized electric-blue triangles and quadrilaterals crossing at roughly 45 degrees. Put a small irregular gold open path near the left edge; no gradient, texture or shadow.

### Ink doodles and stickers

**Use:** Signature human accent beside promises, feature scenes, proof tiles and the closing invitation.

**Observed:** Loose black outlines surround yellow-filled eyes, bulbs, documents and small characters. Irregular rays, motion lines, arrows and loops create a hand-drawn rather than icon-library feel. A 161px-wide drawing balances the opening text at upper right.

**Transfer:** Create a small original metaphor for the new product, with imperfect contours and a single accent fill; a simple outlined tool with radiating marks is a sufficient substitute.

**Build:** Draw SVG paths with rounded joins and deliberately uneven curvature, mostly 2–4px ink strokes. Fill one main shape gold or lilac, add 5–8 short irregular rays, and leave the surrounding ground empty. For the closing scribble, draw one 4px gold zigzag-loop path about 250px wide, clipped at the lower-left edge of the white panel.

### Framed product collage

**Use:** Main feature demonstrations and split product openings.

**Observed:** Crisp white interface fragments sit in chunky rounded frames of blue, sky, lilac or gold. Doodles overlap the frame edges but do not obscure the core UI. Text usually occupies one half and media the other; product-opening objects are roughly 417–448px square.

**Transfer:** Use the new product's real interface, magnified to one understandable action. Without an interface, create a small original workflow with a few labeled steps and the same thick colored backing.

**Build:** Layer a 21–40px-radius color block, a slightly inset white rounded panel, then the interface image with overflow clipped. Add two small offset colored corner tabs or looping paths and one ink doodle above the panel. Keep embellishments flat and sharp, not glossy or volumetric.

### Human demonstration video

**Use:** Hero media and supporting explanation chapters.

**Observed:** Real presenters filmed in naturally lit workspaces, with plants, windows and ordinary furniture. The opening video combines a person with a saturated illustrated explanatory half; supporting recordings can be entirely photographic. Wide media uses a 500px-tall rounded window and centered Playback pill.

**Transfer:** Pair a person explaining a concrete outcome with a simple visual summary. A well-lit still portrait and an adjacent original diagram preserve the tone if video is unavailable.

### Sticker story artwork

**Only for:** story indexes

**Use:** Square gallery covers.

**Observed:** Bright single-color square grounds carry white cut-paper-style logo labels, slightly rotated and outlined in ink, separated by a hand-drawn multiplication mark. Fine loops, dashed paths or corner scribbles add variation.

**Transfer:** Use original customer or project identifiers on irregular white labels; diversify backing colors within a gallery without exporting its entire rainbow to ordinary landing sections.

**Build:** Use a square rounded SVG ground. Center two irregular white label polygons with 2px ink borders, rotate them in opposite directions by a few degrees and separate them with two crossed paths. Add one faint looping path or one corner scribble.

### Icons

**Observed:** Utility icons are compact outline SVGs, commonly 21px with 1.5px rounded strokes in ink. Small chevrons sit next to navigation labels; checks punctuate benefits, and simple arrows mark linked proof.

**Transfer:** Keep functional icons clean and consistent; reserve deliberately irregular strokes for decorative doodles.

## Section recipes

How recurring sections are built.

### First screen: White shell opening

**Observed:** The first 900px layers a full-width Geometric opening backdrop behind a centered Navigation bar and White shell beginning just beneath it. The shell is 1156px wide with 40px corners and 59.5px padding. A sentence-case caption leads a left-aligned 48px/1.25 bold promise on about half the inner width, normally two lines. A small Ink doodle sits at the upper right. The 24.5px support line extends farther across the shell. Primary button and Secondary button sit left, while compact rating marks align to the right of the action row. After a 59.5px gap, a 1037px-wide, 500px-tall human/product video begins. The headline, support, actions, badges and upper portion of the main media are visible within 900px; the media bottom and shell ending fall below the fold.

**Transfer:** Keep the strong shell-and-geometry silhouette, a short outcome promise and the hand-drawn counterweight. Substitute an original workflow diagram beside a presenter or a clear product scene if no video exists. Focused product pages instead open on the pearl canvas with left text and a roughly square illustrated object at right; pricing centers the promise over plans, and story indexes use a plain left title above the gallery.

**Avoid:** Do not replace this with an edge-to-edge dark hero, a giant centered headline, or a full-screen interface image with no white shell.

**Text style:** `display` — Abcwhyte 700 · 48px/1.25

## Page flow

**Storyline:** Outcome promise → tangible demonstration → capability and assurance chapters → customer proof → personal invitation → dark directory, with choice and objection handling added when needed.

### Broad marketing landing page

1. **Promise and product demonstration** — White shell over geometric art, paired actions, ratings and a wide human/product video.
2. **Capability chapters** — Centered introduction, stacked benefit panels and a sky assurance inset.
3. **Social proof** — Wide white two-storey testimonial carousel and logo strip.
4. **Human explanation** — Blue chapter with sky inset and presenter video.
5. **Closing call** — Personal two-column demo form, then dark directory and legal ending.

**Transfer:** Choose a clear promise, explain it visually, then alternate product detail with human evidence.

### Plan selection and detailed evaluation

1. **Choice** — Centered promise above three plans, with blue emphasis on the middle option.
2. **Reassurance and alternatives** — Compact review cards plus wide trial and custom offer panels.
3. **Detailed comparison** — Two-column add-ons and a compact multi-category plan table.
4. **Social proof and objection handling** — Two-storey testimonial strip followed by FAQ and sky contact card.

## Writing

Friendly, reassuring and direct: practical software benefits are expressed as relief from everyday friction. Confidence comes through concrete outcomes, human quotations and short invitations rather than institutional formality.

**Do not copy:** Do not reuse Contractbook or Scrive names, logos, customer marks, proprietary doodle characters, testimonials, ratings, certifications, performance numbers or factual claims. Replace all with original assets and substantiated claims for the new product. The examples below show the pattern only; write new copy for the new product.

- **Addressing the reader:** Speak to the reader as you and your team; use we for the helpful people behind the product. Product references are plain and functional.
- **Rhythm:** Headlines often combine an action with an emotional payoff. Support is usually one or two sentences; short fragments and three-part lists bring energy. Exclamation marks and conversational asides appear sparingly.

| Role | Pattern | Typical length | Examples of the pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main promise | Action or benefit plus an ease/confidence payoff. | 7–14 words | “Control your contracts with confidence & ease” |
| Capability headline | Terse actions followed by a simple payoff. | 3–12 words | “Spot red flags. Rewrite clauses. Summarise terms. Just ask AI Contract Assistant.” |
| Supporting line | Name useful tasks, then compress them into one clear outcome. | 15–30 words | “Everything you need to manage, track, and finalize contracts — all in one clear, actionable space.” |
| Action | Plain verb and next step; assisted and self-serve alternatives. | 2–4 words | “Request a demo”<br>“Start free trial” |
| Proof headline | Short first-person statement with a concrete result. | 6–12 words | “I’ve saved 15-20 hours of manual work.” |
| Closing reassurance | Disarm sales anxiety with a direct, friendly contrast. | 6–14 words | “No pressure—just solutions that make sense.” |

**Words and phrasing to use:** confidence; clarity; peace of mind; all in one place; no more; no fluff; just answers; your goals; smarter; effortlessly

**Avoid:** revolutionary paradigm; enterprise-grade synergy; disrupt the future; unprecedented innovation; legalistic filler

**Rules:**

- Lead with the reader's gain, then explain the mechanism.
- Use conversational contrasts such as no more friction / more control without sounding dismissive.
- Pair numerical claims with genuine supporting proof.
- Give each capability a recognizable task and a human consequence.
- Keep action labels literal; save playful phrasing for headings and supporting reassurance.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep the default headline at 48px/1.25, weight 700 and normal tracking.
- Maintain the 1200px outer container with 22px side padding.
- Give opening shells 40px corners and ordinary proof tiles 24px corners.
- Use 28px for heading-to-copy space and most card gutters.
- Include both bold geometric color and irregular ink decoration in the new design.
- Keep product demonstrations to one understandable action inside a colored rounded frame.
- Pair saturated 280px proof tops with calm explanatory bases.
- Use the yellow hero action and blue full-width form action in their distinct roles.

### Don't

- Do not apply negative display tracking or substitute a condensed headline face.
- Do not make every section electric blue; white and smoke reading areas should dominate.
- Do not add soft floating shadows to ordinary cards; the pricing emphasis is an inset 3px blue line.
- Do not use pill corners on all surfaces; fields remain nearly square at 4.4px.
- Do not turn all small labels into spaced uppercase eyebrows.
- Do not replace the 28px quote treatment with giant 80px quotations; oversized type is reserved for stat cards.
- Do not turn the five-column footer directory into a minimal logo-and-copyright bar.
- Do not apply the story gallery's varied saturated covers as a rainbow background system for ordinary feature text.

## Motion and responsive

### Motion

**Observed:**

- Primary yellow actions declare a 0.4s transition; outlined actions use 0.2s.
- Large playback controls declare background-color 0.2s.
- A logo loop declares 30s linear infinite animation; the strip has white fading edges.

**Transfer:**

- Use the slow continuous logo strip as understated proof motion, retaining faded edges.
- Keep action changes primarily about fill and text color rather than lifting cards.

**Unknown:**

- Scroll-reveal timing, video autoplay behavior and complete focus behavior are not established.

### Responsive

**Observed:**

- Styles declare width breakpoints at 479px, 767px, 768px, 991px, 992px, 1200px, 1440px and 1920px.

**Transfer:**

- On narrow layouts, preserve the white shell and exposed geometric edge while stacking copy before media.
- Stack paired actions and split panels when necessary; keep the doodle adjacent to the promise rather than letting it obscure the text.
- Turn wide proof compositions into a single readable card with neighboring content hinted at; retain the two-storey structure.
- Stack the closing form after reassurance copy and keep the yellow scribble away from fields.

**Unknown:**

- Exact mobile sizes, breakpoint-specific padding and navigation-collapse behavior are not established; stacking guidance is a recommended adaptation.

## How to use this guide

1. Start from the Essentials and the first-screen recipe; where the guide offers variants, use the default named for a new marketing page.
2. Plan the page before building: map your content onto the section recipes and page flow, then check the plan against the Do's and Don'ts.
3. Build with the tokens and component specs; recreate signature graphics with their build recipes instead of generic decoration, and use the named font substitutes if a font is unavailable.
4. Render the page at desktop and mobile widths, compare it with the Essentials and the Don'ts, and fix the differences before you finish.
