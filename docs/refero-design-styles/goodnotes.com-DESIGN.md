# Goodnotes — thinking beyond the margins

## Overview

An open, friendly product style that pairs rounded grotesk typography with the tactile energy of an active working canvas. Mostly white and charcoal, it uses cyan calls to action, pastel-backed interface compositions and faint notebook dots rather than ornamental chrome. It suits collaborative tools and productivity products that can show ideas becoming useful work; specialist variants range from playful turquoise learning stories to black, teal-lit technology showcases.

### Essentials

- A compact promise sits above an oversized working canvas: centered 48px/1.2 semibold type in a 694px text measure, 40px gaps and a 1040px-wide product scene entering the first screen.
- The page behaves like editable paper: faint dot fields surround square, hairline-framed demonstration panels, with 9px white selection handles at corners and joins. Recreate this device with a purpose-built workflow illustration when interface media is unavailable.
- Headings feel rounded but firmly packed: Roobert at 600, -0.02em tracking for 48px and 40px headings, balanced against 18px/1.4 medium-weight explanatory copy.
- Conversion controls are small rounded rectangles, not capsules: cyan fill, 10px corners, 16px/1.25 semibold labels and a small right chevron; reverse to white on cyan bands.
- Color belongs to the work and its staging: keep the ordinary landing page predominantly white, place blue, lavender, butter and navy behind card imagery, and reserve full-width #45bfdb for compact conversion bands.
- Product evidence alternates between expansive scenes and tightly ruled explanations: 900px split demonstrations, 312px text panes with 32px padding, and pastel-topped cards at 310px wide with 28px gutters.
- The ending changes register decisively: a bright centered conversion moment and practical FAQ lead toward a black directory footer with white social circles, a dividing rule and dense 16px navigation columns. The conversion band can occur earlier; preserve the bright moment and the black ending rather than a fixed order.

**Notes:**

- The default is semibold rather than heavy display type. Keep the rounded letterforms and deliberate two-line wrapping; body text is medium, not light.
- Do not merge the normal-tracked FAQ recipe with the -0.02em feature heading, or the -0.05em commercial heading with the ordinary section lead.
- No general viewport-relative type rule is specified; use the desktop recipes as the hierarchy rather than scaling all text with viewport width.

### Surfaces

#### Paper canvas · level 0

**Use:** Default landing-page background.

**CSS:** `background: #ffffff`

**Observed:** White space remains dominant around product evidence.

**Transfer:** Let interface colors supply complexity rather than filling every section.

#### Notebook dot field · level 0

**Use:** Signature backing for feature demonstrations and application grids.

**CSS:** `background-color: #ffffff; background-image: radial-gradient(circle, rgba(30,30,30,0.06) 1.5px, rgba(30,30,30,0) 2px); background-size: 24px 24px`

**Observed:** A very pale, evenly spaced notebook dot texture stretches across the band behind crisp white panels.

**Transfer:** Use this CSS reconstruction as a restrained paper texture; keep dots subordinate to text and media.

#### Selection frame · level 1

**Use:** Signature demonstration frame.

**CSS:** `border: 1px solid rgba(30,30,30,0.29); border-radius: 0px`

**Observed:** Tiny square handles mark corners and split joins; straight lines imply an editable selection rather than a floating browser card.

**Transfer:** Place 9px white squares with 1px translucent charcoal edges on the frame; use a real workflow illustration within it.

#### Cyan conversion band · level 0

**Use:** Bright conversion pause.

**CSS:** `background: #45bfdb; padding: 72px 0px`

**Observed:** Full-bleed cyan supports centered dark copy and a white action.

**Transfer:** Use a compact benefit statement, not a second feature inventory.

#### Black directory · level 0

**Use:** Shared substantial footer.

**CSS:** `background: #000000; padding: 40px 0px`

**Observed:** A social and download strip sits above a thin white divider, then the multi-column directory and a centered copyright line.

**Transfer:** Keep a useful, information-rich ending even when the main page is minimal.

#### Dark capability wash · level 1

**Only for:** dark technology pages

**Use:** Alternating capability matrix on black.

**CSS:** `background: rgba(87,210,238,0.1); border: 1px solid rgba(87,210,238,0.4)`

**Observed:** Translucent cyan reads teal-black; white headings and muted gray paragraphs sit beside dark UI art.

**Transfer:** Retain the straight ruled construction and selection handles; do not turn this into glossy floating cards.

#### Highlighter mark

**Only for:** learning campaigns

**Use:** Single emphasized italic word in a large heading.

**CSS:** `background-image: linear-gradient(rgba(0,0,0,0) 50%, #ffe55f 51%)`

**Observed:** Yellow covers only the lower half of the word, preserving its black silhouette.

**Transfer:** Highlight one meaningful modifier rather than every keyword.

## Imagery and graphics

### Working canvas hero

**Use:** The main opening object and large feature media.

**Observed:** A large, front-facing workspace shows handwriting, diagrams, sticky notes and collected images, usually with a slim blue toolbar. Floating colored cursor-name labels and a loose tool palette break the rectangular boundary. The artwork is broad and legible rather than a tiny full-device mockup.

**Transfer:** Create an equivalent purposeful artifact for the new product, with a few real content clusters and one visible collaborative or editing moment. Minimum acceptable version is a clean SVG workflow or annotated working document, not an empty UI shell.

**Build:** Use a centered white canvas about 1040px wide, a narrow toolbar strip, three asymmetric content clusters, thin connector lines and small pastel note blocks. Add two pointer triangles with rounded name labels and one floating tool strip. Preserve generous empty working space between clusters.

### Pastel media windows

**Use:** Application card and related-feature media headers.

**Observed:** Front-facing interface or document scenes sit inset on blue, lavender, butter or navy fields. Within a 308px by 248px backing, the example artifact is about 265px wide and 175px tall; the surrounding color is clearly visible.

**Transfer:** Frame an actual output or meaningful diagram at the same visual ratio. Keep colors flat and soft rather than turning each backing into a gradient.

**Build:** Create a colored 308px by 248px panel with 10px upper corners; center a 265px-wide white artifact with softly rounded corners, narrow toolbar and a small arrangement of labels, notes or connectors.

### Editable frame handles

**Use:** Signature small marks on demonstration frames and capability rows.

**Observed:** White square nodes, about 9px by 9px with translucent gray outlines, attach to frame corners and where panes divide.

**Transfer:** Repeat this device around key demonstrations even when the product has no literal canvas.

**Build:** Position 9px square spans on a 1px frame, translating each by half its width and height. White fill, 1px rgba(30,30,30,0.3) outline; include nodes at the upper and lower ends of the central divider.

### Security composition

**Use:** Trust and privacy reassurance chapters.

**Observed:** A large green toggle or shield overlays softly blurred files or real workplace photography; foreground access controls remain crisp. The light version sits beside a dashed text frame and certification marks; the dark version shows layered policy windows and a teal-black text panel.

**Transfer:** Use a concrete permissions or protection interaction. Keep the foreground readable and backing diffuse; show only certifications the new product actually holds.

### Teal spark atmosphere

**Only for:** dark technology pages

**Use:** Hero backing around the dark workspace.

**Observed:** Soft cyan-teal four-point spark forms hover at the left and right of a black stage. The sharp center UI has colored notes and a dark translucent generation status bar; atmospheric light remains peripheral.

**Transfer:** Keep the scene black and the product central; use sparse soft sparks rather than a full-page neon gradient. Match the soft glow; exact stops are not essential.

**Build:** Make three SVG four-point stars with curved inward sides, one roughly 180px and two roughly 240px across. Fill with cyan at low opacity, blur by roughly 24px and position at lower-left, upper-middle-left and middle-right behind the main object. Overlay a crisp dark product artifact.

### Playful learning collage

**Only for:** learning campaigns

**Use:** Large cyan openings, benefit chapters and human closing call.

**Observed:** Large black headline at left, a tablet dominating the right, a diagonal white stylus and playful outlined stickers. Oversized yellow and white looping strokes travel behind the scene. Later chapters alternate pale-framed tilted tablets with text; the closing call adds warmly lit human photography.

**Transfer:** Use one large product object and a few subject-relevant hand-drawn stickers. Reserve this energetic collage for an audience-led campaign, not the default landing page.

**Build:** Layer a cyan field, oversized SVG looping paths with rounded joins in yellow and white, then a slightly rotated device or artifact, and three simple outlined sticker illustrations with white offset borders. Keep paths behind text and the main object.

### Icons

**Observed:** Small functional chevrons and arrows accompany navigation and actions. FAQ icons are 24px outline SVGs with 2px rounded strokes; social glyphs are solid dark shapes centered in 40px white circles. Campaign icons are more playful and can sit on yellow tiles.

**Transfer:** Use simple rounded-stroke functional icons for marketing controls; keep playful pictograms inside the learning campaign variant.

## Section recipes

How recurring sections are built.

### First screen: Open working-canvas promise

**Observed:** A 73px white Sticky navigation tops a pure white stage. Center the opening text in a 694px measure, starting 72px below the header; the 48px semibold headline wraps to two lines, followed by a 22px support paragraph and Primary button with 40px gaps. The text stage is about 468px tall. Beneath it, a 1040px-wide Working canvas hero enters with approximately 20px of top breathing room; its toolbar and upper content are visible before the 900px fold, while the lower canvas continues below. Layers remain white paper, then workspace, then floating palette and collaboration labels.

**Transfer:** Use a specific capability promise followed by a convincing artifact of work in progress. Without interface artwork, construct the Working canvas hero as a diagram or annotated document. Overview pages can add a rounded tab rail above the object; commercial pages replace the object with plan choices. Learning campaigns open with a cyan split collage and larger left-aligned type; dark technology pages center white copy above a dark workspace and peripheral teal sparks.

**Avoid:** Do not replace the scene with an abstract gradient blob, add a generic eyebrow above the promise, or use an enormous headline that pushes all product evidence below the fold.

**Text style:** `display` — Roobert 600 · 48px/1.2 · tracking -0.02em

### Ruled interactive explanation

**Observed:** A centered Heading precedes a 900px Selection frame on the Notebook dot field. Expandable demonstration tabs occupy the left half; one active explanation and two compact titles balance an enlarged product detail at right. Small selection handles mark corners and divider joins.

**Transfer:** Use it for closely related capabilities whose differences are best shown through one focused product detail.

**Text style:** `heading` — Roobert 600 · 40px/1.2 · tracking -0.02em

### Application gallery

**Observed:** A centered Heading introduces four Application cards separated by 28px on the Notebook dot field. Each uses Pastel media windows, a short title, a medium-length explanation and a low Arrow link. A narrower related-feature variant uses two wider cards centered together.

**Transfer:** Choose distinct audiences, workflows or outcomes; keep visual artifacts specific to each card.

**Text style:** `heading` — Roobert 600 · 40px/1.2 · tracking -0.02em

### Alternating ruled chapters

**Observed:** A narrow stack of adjacent framed rows alternates Capability text pane and enlarged media. Text occupies 312px and media roughly 554px; rows vary in height with content rather than being forced to identical squares. Handles reinforce the editable frame.

**Transfer:** Explain a few substantial capabilities with readable detail; use the dark capability wash only within a dark technology story.

### Conversational proof track

**Observed:** Centered section title above a horizontally clipped line of Testimonial cards. Long quotes are set at Body size; names and job descriptions are anchored low. Partial cards at the edges signal continuation.

### Bright conversion pause

**Observed:** The Cyan conversion band carries a centered 40px Heading, optional 18px supporting line about 20px below, and a white Primary button about 32px below that. Typical total band height is 263px–330px. Commercial endings instead use a black band with white text and a cyan button.

**Transfer:** State one forward-looking outcome and one next step; this can appear before related features or before the footer.

**Text style:** `heading` — Roobert 600 · 40px/1.2 · tracking -0.02em

**Observed:** The FAQ row list is 920px wide, with a left-aligned Heading and approximately 40px separation before questions. It leads into the Black directory. A top utility strip pairs five social circles with three download badges above a divider; seven desktop directory columns follow, with centered copyright below generous space.

**Transfer:** End with useful answers and navigation, not another oversized illustration. Adapt categories to the new product while preserving the dense black directory contrast.

## Page flow

**Storyline:** Clear promise → visible working artifact → focused capabilities and application examples → human or institutional proof → easy next step → practical answers and a useful directory.

### Broad product overview

1. **Promise** — Centered short claim and cyan action over a tabbed working canvas.

## Writing

Friendly, practical and confidently enabling. The product is presented as a flexible place to do real work, with benefits explained through concrete actions rather than technical bravado.

**Do not copy:** Do not reuse Goodnotes names, logos, proprietary artwork, customer logos, prices, plan benefits, security certifications, factual claims or quotations. Source examples illustrate sentence construction only; replace them with accurate material for the new product.

- **Addressing the reader:** Speak directly to you and your team. Name the product naturally when clarity requires it; explain what it lets the reader do rather than narrating the company's ambitions.
- **Rhythm:** Short benefit headlines, often built from paired verbs or contrasting clauses; support is one or two compact sentences. Longer paragraphs belong in detailed proof and FAQ. Exclamation is occasional, not the default.

| Role | Pattern | Typical length | Examples of the pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero headline | Name the capability and connect it to an expansive benefit. | 6–10 words | “Whiteboard: Infinite space for big ideas”<br>“Simplify teaching, amplify learning” |
| Capability heading | Start with an action or a useful transformation. | 4–8 words | “Turn meeting minutes into a project plan”<br>“Templates to kickstart thinking” |
| Supporting line | Explain the practical movement from input to useful work. | 10–30 words | “Expand your thinking and turn ideas into action”<br>“Designed for every stage of your journey.” |
| Primary action | Low-friction verb and brief destination or offer. | 2–3 words | “Start Free”<br>“Get started” |
| Closing call | A warm invitation or a compact memorable belief. | 5–10 words | “Ideas weren't meant to sit still”<br>“Try [Brand] for your team today” |
| FAQ | Ask a specific reader concern in plain language. | 6–14 words | “Can teams work together live on Whiteboard?” |

**Words and phrasing to use:** ideas; work; connected; create; plan; clarify; turn … into …; pick up where you left off; your team; in real time

**Avoid:** revolutionary paradigm; unparalleled synergy; disrupt everything; technical acronym stacks; manufactured urgency

**Rules:**

- Anchor abstract flexibility in a specific action or work artifact.
- Pair capabilities with the friction they remove, without promising outcomes the product cannot support.
- Use sentence case and conversational contractions; keep labels literal.
- Let parallel verbs and short contrasting clauses provide momentum instead of excessive punctuation.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep opening copy within a 694px centered measure before introducing the wider media.
- Use 48px/1.2 semibold opening type and 40px/1.2 section leads as the ordinary hierarchy.
- Put the dot field and selection handles around meaningful demonstrations.
- Use 28px gutters between 310px application cards and align their arrow links low.
- Keep 32px padding in ruled capability text panes.
- Use white controls on the full-width cyan conversion band.
- Maintain 24px FAQ questions with thin bottom separators.
- End with black navigation density and white social circles, even on a very light page.

### Don't

- Do not turn the default 10px action radius into a capsule.
- Do not use 72px learning-campaign type as the ordinary centered landing-page headline.
- Do not place drop shadows under every marketing card; hairline outlines do the structural work.
- Do not spread pastel color through the entire card body; it belongs mainly behind media.
- Do not use pale low-weight body copy; the default is 18px at weight 500.
- Do not merge all straight-edged demonstration panes into rounded floating tiles.
- Do not use the green comparison panel outside its audience-campaign context.
- Do not shrink detailed comparison tables into the same airy typography as feature storytelling.

## Motion and responsive

### Motion

**Observed:**

- Primary buttons declare a 0.3s transition; their cyan fill darkens on hover.
- Arrow links declare 0.2s ease-in-out transitions.
- Expandable demonstration tabs declare height 0.3s ease-in-out; application cards declare transform 0.3s.
- Repeated horizontal tracks include a declared 28s linear infinite marquee animation.

**Transfer:**

- Use the declared height change to exchange emphasis between a compact title row and its full explanation.
- Keep movement attached to the product explanation or horizontal proof track rather than animating every paragraph.

**Unknown:**

- Exact entrance choreography and timing of product scenes are not specified.

### Responsive

**Observed:**

- Styles declare width breakpoints at 376px, 479px, 767px, 768px, 991px, 1200px and 1440px; pricing additionally declares 1100px, and the dark variant 992px.

## How to use this guide

1. Start from the Essentials and the first-screen recipe; where the guide offers variants, use the default named for a new marketing page.
2. Plan the page before building: map your content onto the section recipes and page flow, then check the plan against the Do's and Don'ts.
3. Build with the tokens and component specs; recreate signature graphics with their build recipes instead of generic decoration, and use the named font substitutes if a font is unavailable.
4. Render the page at desktop and mobile widths, compare it with the Essentials and the Don'ts, and fix the differences before you finish.
