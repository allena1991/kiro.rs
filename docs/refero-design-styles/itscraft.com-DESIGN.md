# Craft — cultivated light and flare

## Overview

An editorial, organic style with tall flared letterforms, expressive slanted headlines and luminous botanical imagery. Warm stone backgrounds and forest green stages are punctuated by electric green actions, while photography supplies the richer pinks, yellows and purples. It suits human-centered services and ambitious products that want cultural confidence rather than software sterility; the compositions are spacious, but the information grids are tightly packed.

### Essentials

- Headlines behave like two voices in conversation: light condensed flared capitals at 182px/0.85 and weight 300, with an upright first phrase and a right-anchored slanted reply; smaller feature pairs use 48px/0.95.
- The opening is an immersive green field rather than a centered sales stack: a 900px-tall forest stage, a luminous blurred organic form in the upper right, a headline spanning nearly the full width, and a 304px-wide support paragraph low on the left.
- Actions are bright little tabs, not capsules: electric green #26d862, forest text, 200×40px desktop size, 8px corners and 24px horizontal padding; secondary editorial actions use 4px corners and a fine ink outline.
- Color lives in imagery and deliberate stages: warm #eae6df dominates the reading sections, #1d3023 frames the opening and a feature band, and electric green is limited to actions, inline emphasis and atmospheric light rather than whole reading sections.
- Images are tactile specimens, not pictograms: tall 2:3 macro photographs with soft motion blur, grain and saturated studio backings; arrange 304×456px panels with 8px gutters. For non-botanical products, use equally close studies of materials or a softly lit physical detail.
- The ending is a brand-scale light installation: a centered action and upright/slanted 48px phrase above an enormous pale wordmark over grainy green light. Carry this device into every new page using the new identity's lettering, with a simple thin outlined wordmark as the minimum version.

## Typography

- **ABC Arizona Flare Variable** — Paragraphs, labels, navigation and actions. Humanist sans foundation with gentle flared terminals; unusually light text weight gives an editorial warmth. If unavailable, use **Alegreya Sans**. Use light and regular weights; preserve compact spacing and soft terminal character. Font stack: "ABC Arizona Flare Variable", "Alegreya Sans", sans-serif.
- **ABC Arizona Flare Condensed Variable** — Display, headings, statistics, lists and card titles. Tall narrow flared forms with a serif-like display presence; light upright capitals alternate with deeply slanted phrases. If unavailable, use **Instrument Serif**. Use regular and italic; tune width to the narrow display proportions. Font stack: "ABC Arizona Flare Condensed Variable", "Instrument Serif", serif.
- **fort-condensed** (only for people directories and location selectors) — Tiny uppercase geographic tags and floating names. Dense, bold condensed grotesk that contrasts with the light flared display. If unavailable, use **Barlow Condensed**. Use 700 and 800. Font stack: "fort-condensed", "Barlow Condensed", sans-serif.

**Notes:**

- Keep the text weight light even when scale becomes monumental. The display's flared construction, not boldness, supplies character.
- The slanted voice uses the variable font's slnt axis at -14; use a genuine italic in the substitute. Pair it with an upright phrase, never slant all copy.
- Large display and stat lines compress to 0.85; descriptive copy opens to 1.25. Small uppercase section headings retain normal tracking rather than adopting widely spaced luxury labels.

## Layout

**Density:** Airy sections with tightly knit grids: large visual fields and broad pauses coexist with 8px gutters.

### Spacing

| Value | Use |
| --- | --- |

| 12px | Card heading-to-description gap and label offset below section rule. |
| 16px | Outer minimum side padding, media overlays and section top padding. |
| 24px | Stat card padding, action side padding and intro stack gaps. |
| 32px | Section heading bottom space and service card bottom padding. |
| 40px | Service row gaps and testimonial text-to-attribution gap. |

| 64px | Interior introduction padding and larger section bottoms. |

### Containers and grids

- **content-grid**: max width 1240px · 12 columns · gap 8px · side padding 16px. Centered desktop content grid. At 1440px the content edges sit 100px from either side; the 16px padding is the minimum outer gutter.
- **four-column**: 4 columns · gap 8px. Four 304px-wide proof or image cards.
- **editorial-pairs**: 2 columns · gap 8px. Two 616px-wide horizontal article cards; row gap 16px.
- **intro-column**: max width 616px. Centered interior headline and floating navigation width.
- **feature-heading**: max width 408px. Compact centered title block with left upright text and right slanted conclusion.
- **three-column** (only for expanded service catalogs): 3 columns · gap 8px. Three 408px-wide service cards with tall images; wrap to further rows.

## Imagery and graphics

### Luminous organic opening

**Use:** Signature atmosphere behind the opening headline.

**Observed:** An abstract folded leaf or flower-like shape occupies the upper-right region on deep green. Cream light blooms at its center; soft green folds, long radial smears and fine grain keep it photographic rather than vector-sharp. The headline remains in front.

**Transfer:** Make a close, abstract study of a material relevant to the product, with a luminous focal point and dark green negative space. Minimum version: a blurred cream starburst above overlapping green ellipses, with fine noise.

**Build:** On the forest field place three overlapping SVG elliptical petals, approximately 600×360px, rotated -20, 25 and 75 degrees around a point in the upper-right quadrant. Fill with forest-to-leaf gradients. Add 5 tapered ivory rays radiating from that point, with 12–28px Gaussian blur and varied alpha. Overlay monochrome turbulence noise at about 0.08 alpha; keep the brightest core away from the main letter shapes.

### Botanical specimens

**Use:** Capability galleries and atmospheric editorial backdrops.

**Observed:** Flowers are photographed extremely close: pink petals, vivid yellow heads, purple blooms and translucent pale leaves against forest, lilac or mint studio backings. Some are sharp at the center with radial motion trails. Portrait 2:3 framing and 8px corners make them feel like collected specimens; the images take much more space than their captions.

**Transfer:** Use close studies of the new product's materials, craft or physical effects, with one clear object per frame. Preserve varied bright backing colors, shallow focus and occasional motion softness rather than switching to generic iconography.

### Grainy closing wordmark

**Use:** Signature closing scene on every page.

**Observed:** A huge thin pale wordmark spreads across most of the lower canvas, backed by green blurred waves and a darker central trough. The image bleeds beyond the frame and fades into the warm background above. The small action and paired heading float well above the giant letters.

**Transfer:** Use the new identity's wordmark as an SVG or thin typographic silhouette, almost the width of the viewport. Preserve its readability and luminous backing; the minimum version is thin light lettering over a few softly blended green fields.

**Build:** Layer broad green radial gradients at the lower-left and lower-right, a forest oval in the bottom center, and warm-canvas transparency above. Blur the light fields by 60–100px and overlay fine monochrome noise at about 0.06 alpha. Place the ivory wordmark above them at roughly 75% of viewport width and a 2.5:1 width-to-height silhouette; mask an optional canvas-to-ivory gradient through the letters. Match the soft glow; exact stops are not essential.

### Editorial edge bloom

**Use:** Atmospheric counterweight beside a centered editorial card row.

**Observed:** An enormous abstract floral detail is pushed mostly outside the left edge: pink, pale blue and warm yellow haze enters the canvas without a rectangular frame.

**Transfer:** Enlarge and heavily soften one specimen image so only a fraction is visible, leaving the card content crisp.

**Build:** Position a roughly 1750px square specimen image mostly beyond the left edge, using a radial alpha mask to fade it into the canvas. If no image exists, overlap translucent rose, pale blue and ivory ellipses with 70px blur; keep them subordinate to the readable card row.

### People portraits

**Only for:** company and team pages

**Use:** People cards and leadership proof.

**Observed:** Relaxed three-quarter-length studio portraits, usually dark or neutral clothing, retain natural skin color. Grainy green and pale lilac luminous backdrops connect them to the abstract brand art. Consistent portrait ratios contrast with spontaneous gestures and expressions.

**Transfer:** Shoot real people with consistent soft studio light; composite onto a restrained green atmospheric backing if a shared physical set is unavailable.

### Event reportage

**Only for:** community campaigns and article archives

**Use:** Show real participation and shared energy.

**Observed:** Color photographs of speakers with handheld microphones, seated audiences and conversations in lived-in venues. Images crop tightly to people; opening galleries vary their heights and extend beyond the screen.

**Transfer:** Choose candid participation rather than posed corporate handshakes; use real lighting and keep the energetic asymmetrical gallery.

### Campaign title and corner light

**Only for:** community campaigns

**Use:** A distinct initiative identity within the shared site.

**Observed:** Decorative rose calligraphic lettering is centered on plum, with a pale blue and pink blurred ribbon entering from the upper-left edge. The title is artwork rather than the normal text family.

**Transfer:** Create an original compact decorative title mark for the initiative; do not replace the default site's condensed type with script everywhere.

**Build:** For the corner effect, layer two wide curved SVG ribbons entering from outside the top-left: a pale blue arc and a rose arc, each fading to transparent before the center. Apply 35px blur and a low-alpha noise layer. Keep the central title area dark and clear.

### Icons

**Observed:** Icons are sparse: a narrow filled 9×14px navigation glyph, a small stacked-line menu mark in a 40px square, and 32px carousel arrows with 1.5px strokes and butt caps. Their colors follow the surrounding text.

**Transfer:** Choose small plain directional or media utility marks; avoid a decorative feature-icon system.

## Section recipes

How recurring sections are built.

### First screen: Immersive flared opening

**Observed:** The first 900px is one full-height Forest stage. Floating navigation sits high and centered; Luminous organic opening fills the upper right behind the words. A giant upright first phrase begins near the left edge across about two thirds of the width, and a second slanted phrase answers beneath it, anchored to the right. Both use 182px capitals with 0.85 line height, forming a roughly 309px-tall two-line composition through the middle. A short body paragraph is low in the left-center grid, 304px wide, with 32px breathing room above the bottom. The first screen contains navigation, both headline phrases, the atmospheric form and support copy; it contains no in-hero conversion button. The warm thesis section begins below the fold.

**Transfer:** Make the promise two complementary phrases with contrasting upright and slanted voices. Without bespoke art, use the blurred-petal light recipe rather than inserting a product screenshot into a centered card. Service and company introductions instead open on Warm canvas with a centered 616px-wide 72px title pair, 64px vertical padding and optional support/action rows 24px apart. Campaign introductions use Campaign stage, a compact decorative title, centered 440px copy and an overflowing reportage gallery.

**Avoid:** Do not shrink the opening into a conventional centered headline/subhead/button stack or put a hard-edged illustration beside it.

**Text style:** `display` — ABC Arizona Flare Condensed Variable 300 · 182px/0.85 · tracking -0.025em · uppercase

## Page flow

**Storyline:** Immersive promise → positioning → evidence and capabilities → deeper human or editorial proof → concentrated offer → luminous closing signature.

### Broad marketing landing page

1. **Promise** — Full-height forest atmosphere and monumental two-voice headline.
2. **Positioning** — Centered linked thesis, small ruled label and broad introductory prose.
3. **Quantified proof** — Four tall pale statistic cards.
4. **Capabilities** — Four specimen image cards followed by a centered action.
5. **Editorial proof** — Paired heading and three portrait stories.
6. **Focused offer** — Dark resource feature with a large cover and download action.
7. **Closing call** — One action, a paired promise and huge glowing wordmark.

**Transfer:** Use the atmospheric opening as the default for a new landing page; choose proof and capability blocks appropriate to the new offer.

### Community or initiative campaign

1. **Identity and invitation** — Plum panel, decorative title, concise mission and inline registration link.
2. **Participation proof** — Overflowing candid gallery, followed by centered rationale and two supporting columns.
3. **Program archive** — Giant typographic event links within the dark panel.
4. **Editorial continuation** — Warm-canvas article grid and ruled related initiative links.
5. **Closing call** — Shared green-light wordmark ending.

**Transfer:** Keep the alternate campaign palette local to the initiative; use the shared editorial and closing language to retain family resemblance.

### Detailed service conversion page

1. **Promise** — Compact warm introduction with upright/slanted headline, support line and two actions.
2. **Offer detail** — Lead prose and a six-item specimen service catalog.
3. **Method** — Numbered ruled process rows.
4. **Social proof** — Oversized partner-name rows and forest-backed quotation carousel.
5. **Human and quantified proof** — Leadership portraits followed by the statistic row.
6. **Capability depth** — Half-width category and stacked specialism lists.
7. **Contact access** — Monumental location selector and floating contact card.
8. **Closing call** — Shared luminous sign-off.

**Transfer:** For a complex service, combine emotional imagery with specific process and expertise; use the quieter introduction rather than repeating the immersive hero.

### Company and people page

1. **Point of view** — Warm title pair and broad editorial introduction.
2. **Operating principles** — Horizontal ivory cards over a soft photographic field.
3. **Human proof** — Four-column portrait directory with roles, small geography tags and direct actions.
4. **Contact access** — Oversized city selector with colored person markers.
5. **Community proof** — Two-column editorial initiative grid.
6. **Closing call** — Same large wordmark and atmospheric green ending.

**Transfer:** Let people, practice and participation substantiate the point of view; scale directory length to the new organization.

## Writing

Warm, cultivated and assured, with an editorial interest in people and possibility. Confidence comes from concrete outcomes and attentive language rather than aggressive selling.

**Do not copy:** Do not reuse Craft's name, logos, campaign marks, botanical assets, client names, quotations, founding date, statistics or recruitment claims as the new product's own. Source examples illustrate rhythm only.

- **Addressing the reader:** Speak directly to 'you' and describe the organization as 'we'. Make the relationship collaborative rather than positioning the reader as a lead to be processed.
- **Rhythm:** Short, resonant headlines often split into an assertion and a reply. Supporting sentences are conversational and concrete; longer paragraphs use short clauses and deliberate repeated words.

| Role | Pattern | Typical length | Examples of the pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero headline | An evocative active phrase about discovery or growth, with a responding second phrase. | 3–10 words | “Unearthing what's next”<br>“Nurturing talent is our second nature” |
| Supporting line | A reader-centered condition followed by a humane promise. | 10–25 words | “If you create with care, you deserve to grow with care.”<br>“When the right people meet the right conditions, growth is inevitable.” |
| Section label | Plain descriptive navigation, often collective rather than promotional. | 3–6 words | “Ways we support your growth”<br>“We work with the best” |
| Card heading | A short outcome or opportunity stated without hype. | 2–5 words | “Your next chapter”<br>“Possibility demands curiosity” |
| Proof explanation | A short interpretive lead sentence followed by a specific contextual fact. | 20–40 words | “Relationships, built to last. Most of our work is with agency and brand partners who we’ve worked with for over two years.” |
| Primary action | Direct verb and object, without sales pressure. | 1–4 words | “Contact us”<br>“Download”<br>“Find a freelancer” |

**Words and phrasing to use:** care; possibility; growth; what's next; shared momentum; create; conditions; connections; curiosity

**Avoid:** disrupt everything; 10x; game-changing; unlock your potential!!!; dominate; frictionless ecosystem

**Rules:**

- Use one coherent organic metaphor at a time, supported by literal language about the offer.
- Pair warmth with proof: explain what a result means, not just how impressive it sounds.
- Let repetitions create cadence, especially paired words such as care/create or talent/talent.
- Keep button labels literal even when the surrounding headline is poetic.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep the main display at weight 300 with 0.85 line height and a contrasting slanted reply.
- Use 8px gutters inside the 1240px content grid.
- Use 18px/1.25 support copy rather than tiny editorial text beneath large images.
- Anchor stat explanations to the bottom of 380px cards.
- Keep the green action at 200×40px on desktop with 8px corners.
- Use 1px #d7d2cc rules to introduce and divide information.
- Stage tall imagery at 2:3 with 8px corners and tactile color.
- Give the giant closing wordmark enough green light and pale negative space to read as artwork.

### Don't

- Do not use bold geometric sans headlines in place of the light flared letterforms.
- Do not turn every button into a pill; 9999px rounding belongs to tiny metadata tags.
- Do not apply drop shadows to ordinary pale cards; they are flat tonal sheets.
- Do not use electric green as the background behind long reading passages.
- Do not widen 8px card gutters to a generic 24px dashboard grid.
- Do not make all imagery dark monochrome; bright specimen colors and natural skin tones are central.
- Do not replace 16px normal-tracked section labels with widely tracked miniature mono captions.
- Do not make campaign plum, decorative calligraphy or colored people markers the default style of an ordinary landing page.

## Motion and responsive

### Motion

**Observed:**

- Editorial actions declare color-related transitions of 0.15s linear and a 3% ink hover wash.
- Some actions and surfaces declare all-property transitions of 0.1s cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1).
- Display reveal wrappers use clipping and transforms; large partner-name rows use horizontal translated positions. The navigation persists during scrolling, and location names have selected and faded states.

**Transfer:**

- Preserve the clipped two-phrase headline reveal without making the support paragraph compete for attention.
- Use restrained color changes for small actions; keep the atmospheric art and large typographic selectors as the expressive motion surfaces.

**Unknown:**

- Hero artwork playback, scroll-linked light behavior and headline reveal timing are not specified.
- Focus-visible and active styling beyond the listed declarations is unknown.

### Responsive

**Observed:**

- Declared width breakpoints include 352px, 425px, 440px, 480px, 576px, 600px, 767px, 768px, 782px, 845px and 960px.
- Interior title classes specify 40px below desktop and 72px at desktop; feature heading classes specify 40px then 48px. Introductory prose specifies 24px then 32px.
- Primary action classes specify 56px height before desktop and 40px at desktop; portrait media rows use horizontal snap behavior and 80% card widths before desktop.
- People card classes change from a horizontal wrapped layout to a vertical desktop card.

**Transfer:**

- On narrow screens preserve upright-versus-slanted hierarchy while using the smaller title sizes, rather than reducing the slanted reply to body text.
- Retain portrait card proportions and use a horizontally scrollable story rail where multiple full-height images cannot fit.
- Keep the closing wordmark oversized and clipped to the viewport, with the green light concentrated below the readable closing copy.

**Unknown:**

- Actual narrow-screen compositions and how all breakpoint rules combine are not established.
- The desktop hero's complete mobile type sizing and artwork placement are unspecified.

## How to use this guide

1. Start from the Essentials and the first-screen recipe; where the guide offers variants, use the default named for a new marketing page.
2. Plan the page before building: map your content onto the section recipes and page flow, then check the plan against the Do's and Don'ts.
3. Build with the tokens and component specs; recreate signature graphics with their build recipes instead of generic decoration, and use the named font substitutes if a font is unavailable.
4. Render the page at desktop and mobile widths, compare it with the Essentials and the Don'ts, and fix the differences before you finish.
